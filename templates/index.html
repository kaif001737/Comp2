Message from the Headmistress

Cultivating Minds, Inspiring Hearts

Welcome to St. Antony's Girls Hr. Sec. School. For over seven decades, our school has stood as a beacon of educational excellence in Chennai. We believe in providing an empowering environment where every young girl is encouraged to explore her potential, voice her ideas, and develop into a confident leader.

Our focus extends beyond classrooms; we blend classical moral foundations with modern scientific inquiry. Here, girls do not just receive degrees, they acquire character, build empathy, and establish friendships that last a lifetime.

"We do not train students merely to pass public examinations, but to navigate the path of life with grace, knowledge, and courage."

Sr. Dr. Margaret Daisy
Sr. Dr. Margaret Daisy
Headmistress, St. Antony's Girls Hr. Sec. School
From the Correspondent's Desk

Guiding with Faith, Vision & Dedication

At St. Antony's Girls Hr. Sec. School, our sacred calling is to nurture young girls into spiritually grounded, intellectually accomplished, and morally courageous citizens. Rooted in our Christian heritage and values of love, truth, and service, we are committed to providing accessible and high-quality education to all.

As a government-aided institution, we uphold the sacred trust of shaping future generations with dedicated teachers, modern facilities, and compassionate guidance. May St. Antony shower his blessings upon every child who enters our portal.

"Guided by divine light and truth, every student here is empowered to serve society with wisdom, integrity, and grace."
